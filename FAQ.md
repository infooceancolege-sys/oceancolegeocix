# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What is Ocean College?
Ocean College is an institution focused on marine sciences, offering a variety of courses and programs to prepare students for careers in marine research, conservation, and education.

## 2. How can I apply to Ocean College?
You can apply online through our website or by contacting the admissions office for more information.

## 3. What programs are offered at Ocean College?
We offer programs in marine biology, oceanography, marine conservation, and more. Check our website for a full list of programs and courses.

## 4. What facilities does Ocean College provide?
Ocean College provides state-of-the-art laboratories, research vessels, and field study opportunities to give students hands-on experience in marine sciences.

## 5. Are there any scholarships available?
Yes, Ocean College offers several scholarships based on merit and need. Please visit our scholarships page for more details.

## 6. Can I visit Ocean College before applying?
Absolutely! We encourage prospective students to schedule a campus visit to learn more about our programs and facilities.

## 7. What career opportunities are available for graduates?
Our graduates have pursued careers in research, conservation, education, policy-making, and more across various organizations and agencies.
